Proactive outreach · the greeting bubble speaks up when a rule matches what the visitor is doing

      
Rules are checked in priority order (lowest number first) when a page loads, and again when a time-on-page or scroll threshold a rule waits for is reached. One rule bubble per page view; a visitor who closes a rule bubble or opens the chat is left alone for the rest of the visit. Each rule fires at most once per cooldown per device. Counts are for the period selected above.

      
    

    

      
Visitor context · returning visitors who never chatted

      
A browser is recognised by its device id only, never by IP. The IP only looks up the organisation or country of the current network; it is not stored. A company is named only after two visits from its network, or once you approve it below. Nothing happens for GPC/DNT browsers or (by default) EU/UK visitors.

      
      
Off on the server: OUTREACH_IP_CONTEXT or TRACKING_ENABLED is false.
